Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What this app stores

This music player has no accounts, no sign in, no analytics and no ads. It does not create a profile of you. Your liked songs, playlists, recently played list and theme choice are saved only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to an account or a database. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Services it talks to

  • YouTube: songs play in the official embedded YouTube player. YouTube can collect data as described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube search: when you search, your search words are sent from the local server to YouTube to get results.
  • Listen together: when you start or join a session, the name you type, the session code, the song you are playing and its position are passed through the server to the other listeners. The server keeps this in memory only and forgets it when the session ends.
  • Playback check: the server asks YouTube whether a song may be played outside YouTube, and looks for another upload when it may not.
  • Google sign in (optional): if you sign in, Google gives this page read only access to your YouTube playlists. The access token stays in your browser memory, is not saved, and is not sent to our server. Signing out revokes it.
  • Artists and playlists: the artist names and song lists you open are looked up from YouTube Music by the server.
  • LrcLib: when a song starts, its title, artist and length are sent to lrclib.net to find lyrics.
